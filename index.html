SMALL ACTIONS. MORE POSSIBILITY.

Time Returned

Give Obi his day back.

Every day, Obi walks three hours for water. Collect supplies, build a well, and give him time to go to school.

Tap rope, coins, and clean water for +1 item each. Avoid muddy puddles: they cost 2 items. Each day lasts 20 seconds. Between days, spend items on a rain barrel (8), a filter (12), then a well (20). Build the well by the end of day 10. Use Tab and Enter to play with a keyboard.
